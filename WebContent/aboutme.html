Purple Pikmin

Purple Pikmin are heavier and stronger than other types. They can deal significant damage and carry heavy objects due to their weight, but they move slower than other Pikmin.

Rock Pikmin

Rock Pikmin have a sturdy appearance. They can break through crystal barriers and cannot be crushed or impaled, making them effective against certain obstacles.

Winged Pikmin

Winged Pikmin resemble small bees and can fly. They can hover over water and reach elevated areas, allowing them to retrieve items that other types cannot access.
